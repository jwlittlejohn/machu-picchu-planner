    Getting there

    • Toronto: Air Canada and Air Transat both fly nonstop to Lima, about eight hours. Air Canada's Toronto flights look seasonal, starting in October, so August nonstops are likely Air Transat.
    • Ottawa: Air Canada is making Montréal to Lima year-round from summer 2027, so Ottawa to Montréal to Lima is a clean one-stop.
    • Budget: August round trips from Toronto average about $970. Allow $1,000 to $1,200 each, more from Ottawa.
    • One-way endings: three of the five trips end in Cusco. Either book a Cusco to Lima flight to meet the flight home, or add a night in Lima.
    • Summer 2027 schedules weren't final when this was written. Check flight days against the tour's start date before paying a deposit.

    Before booking

    • Sale deadlines: G Adventures ends December 4, Intrepid December 6. G's largest discount covers departures only to April 30, 2027, so ask what applies in August.
    • Six seats on one departure is the real constraint. Intrepid groups stop at 12. Phone rather than booking online, so all six are held together, and ask about a group rate.
    • Confirm the August price for the two G Adventures trips on the Canadian site. The figures here are converted from US list prices.
    • Trekkers: Inca Trail permits are limited and not guaranteed. Book early and name the trekkers when booking.
    • Health: see a travel clinic a couple of months ahead about altitude and the Amazon. Travel insurance is compulsory on G Adventures.
    • Passports are needed for Machu Picchu tickets and Inca Trail permits. Use the same passport you'll travel on.
